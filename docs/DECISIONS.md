# Atlas Decision Log

- **D001 Repository-first:** structured repository data is source of truth; spreadsheets are generated outputs.
- **D002 Product-centric:** products are canonical; seller listings are child records.
- **D003 Immutable IDs:** canonical products use stable IDs such as `ATL-000001`.
- **D004 No community star rating:** preserve community signals instead of compressing them.
- **D005 Facts separated from interpretations.**
- **D006 Verification types instead of one universal badge.**
- **D007 Negative knowledge is first-class data.**
- **D008 Images require provenance.**
- **D009 Methodology may change when stronger evidence appears.**
