# Atlas Confidence Framework

Atlas separates **quality** from **confidence**.

## Coverage dimensions
Listing identity, current price, seller identity, warehouse QC, measurements, weight/material, fit, batch/factory, community reports, durability, and alternatives/duplicates.

Each dimension may be `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `unknown`.

## Overall confidence v0.1
- Evidence directness and independence — 40%
- Listing health and recency — 20%
- Manufacturing/QC consistency — 15%
- Independent community corroboration — 15%
- Research completeness — 10%

## Bands
- high: 80–100
- medium: 60–79
- low: 40–59
- insufficient: 0–39

The number is a research-confidence score, not the probability the product is good.
